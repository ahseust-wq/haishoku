Haishoku privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · App: Haishoku: Sanzo Wada Colors (com.seus.haishoku)

Who we are

Haishoku is published by Ahsen Serkan Usta, an independent developer based in Germany, who is the controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: REPLACE@EMAIL, postal address: REPLACE WITH ADDRESS.

What stays on your device

  • Saved combinations, theme choice and quiz score are stored only on your phone. We have no server and no user accounts.
  • Photos you open in Lens are analysed on your phone and never uploaded, stored or shared. A photo taken with the camera is kept in the app's private cache and overwritten by the next one.
  • Images you share are created on your phone and passed to the app you choose in the share sheet.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first through Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time under Learn → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Haishoku Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we receive only a confirmation that the purchase exists, never your payment details.

Fonts

The app loads two typefaces from Google Fonts. This request reveals your IP address to Google. No cookies are set by it.

Legal bases

Personalised advertising relies on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and font delivery rely on our legitimate interest in funding and running the app (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. As we hold no personal data on servers, most requests concern Google; we will help you direct them. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. You may complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Haishoku is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.
